    Privacy & Terms

    Privacy Policy

    Data Storage: All phone numbers, names, and messages are stored locally on your device (LocalStorage). We have no access to this data. Note: Clearing your browser cache or website data will permanently delete your recents and saved contacts.

    Analytics: We use anonymous analytics to track country-level traffic. No personal information or IP addresses are stored or linked to you.

    Terms of Service

    Service: FlashPing uses WhatsApp's official Click to Chat feature. We do not host any messaging services.

    Non-Affiliation: This tool is NOT affiliated with, authorized, or endorsed by WhatsApp Inc. or Meta.

    Disclaimer: Use this tool at your own risk. We are not responsible for any misuse or consequences of messages sent via this tool.

    Install FlashPing

    To install this app on your iPhone:

    1. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow) at the bottom of the screen.
    2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
    3. Tap Add in the top-right corner.
